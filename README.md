## What I build

I work across the full data stack — from production ELT pipelines and Snowflake data marts 
to multi-agent AI systems and fine-tuned LLMs.

At IQVIA, I built automated pharmaceutical data pipelines that cut processing from 10 hours 
to 10 minutes and eliminated 250+ monthly manual hours across cross-functional teams.

At Northeastern, I pushed into the AI layer — fine-tuning Llama 3.1 on clinical notes, 
building a knowledge graph for clinical trial matching using SNOMED CT and Neo4j, and 
architecting a 4-agent LangGraph advisory platform integrating live financial and 
geographic data.
